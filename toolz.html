Tejiri Outreach

Early access

A command-line tool for selling a domain through careful, one-to-one email. It works from a buyer list you've researched, finds each business's published contact email, checks it, picks the best contact, and sends a short personalized sequence from your own mailbox. When someone replies or asks to stop, it stops.

What it does

  • Extract: published emails from each prospect's own website
  • Verify: syntax and mail servers; flags generic and free-mail addresses
  • Sort: keeps the best contact per business
  • Send: a 6-step sequence with same-thread follow-ups
  • Inbox: reads replies, opt-outs and bounces automatically

Built-in limits

  • At most 40 emails a day per inbox (15 by default)
  • Minutes between emails, business hours only
  • No first email without a personal line you write
  • Postal address and opt-out line in every email
  • A permanent do-not-contact list

You'll need

  • Python 3.11 or newer
  • Your own email account with SMTP and IMAP access
  • A researched list of businesses (CSV or Google Sheet export)
  • A free GitHub account

It's not built for bulk email, purchased lists or scraping the web, and it won't do those things.
